English

Permissions: the app asks for no special permissions.

Purchases and ads: this version has none. If they are added in the future, this policy will be updated before that release, including the data the advertising provider processes and how to manage consent.

Children: the game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Questions or a request to delete data: write to 7448214@gmail.com.
